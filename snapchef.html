Next.js + AI · Food assistant

SnapChef

Turn a food photo into recipe ideas, ingredient suggestions, and nutrition estimates, with saved scans and account history.

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Supabase · AI APIs

Open app ↗View repository ↗

Recipes from food photos

Upload a food photo, choose servings, and add dietary or equipment preferences. SnapChef returns recipe ideas, ingredients, cooking steps, substitutions, and shopping estimates.

Analysis and saved recipes

A Next.js server route sends the image to the configured AI provider and keeps the API key on the server. Supabase handles sign-in and saved recipes, which can be reopened from My Scans.

Uploads and errors

JPEG, PNG, and WebP uploads share a 3 MB limit across the client and API. Invalid files, provider failures, and timeouts show an error with an option to try again.

Components and tests

Upload, account, history, and result views have separate components. Automated tests cover invalid uploads, failed requests, saved history, cleanup after failed saves, and sign-out behavior. AI and Supabase calls are mocked in tests.

Estimates and photo storage

Ingredients, allergens, nutrition, and prices cannot be verified from a photo alone. The built-in example is labeled. Saved recipe rows are private to their owner, but saved photos currently use a public bucket and can be viewed by anyone with their URL.
