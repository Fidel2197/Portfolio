Python + React · Data quality

DataDock

A spreadsheet can look fine until you try to use it. DataDock helps you find missing values and duplicate rows, explore the data, and choose what to clean before exporting. Accounts keep reports available across devices.

The work goes beyond charts: Python analysis, report ownership, useful upload errors, and tests that check account isolation.

Next.js + AI · Food assistant

SnapChef

Start with a food photo and work toward something you can cook. SnapChef combines recipe ideas, substitutions, rough prices, and preferences such as budget or kitchen equipment. Sign in to save a recipe for later.

The focus is the whole flow: upload, review, refine, and save—with clear limits on what AI can tell from a photo.

Next.js · Incident practice

SignalDesk

A workspace for deciding what needs attention and what to do next. Work through simulated incidents, update their status, and follow a runbook. Progress stays in your browser; separate checks retrieve real public service status.

A refresh used to lose status changes. The current version saves them and tests that behavior.
