My approach

How I build.

A few notes on planning, testing, and the tools I use.

Planning

I sketch the screens and decide what the app needs to save before building the first version.

Testing

I test uploads, failed requests, sign-in, saved data, and refresh behavior. DataDock includes account-isolation tests; SnapChef and SignalDesk test their main user actions.

Fixes from feedback

I fixed DataDock’s clipped sidebar and moved its sign-in controls. In SignalDesk, I added browser storage so incident changes survive a refresh.

AI assistance

I use AI tools for coding, refactoring, documentation, and tests. I direct the features and design, review the changes, and check the result.
